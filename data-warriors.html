Every day, log five learning or practice entries for each active skill category. For every entry, choose Practice or Learn, note what you worked on, and add a link where you have one.

Start small.
Build momentum. Level up.

The first 10 days are your consistency milestone. Clear them, and the next level opens.

LEVEL 1Open to everyone

Data Warriors

For your first 10 consecutive challenge days:

  • Log five learning or practice entries per active skill category.
  • Apply to at least five relevant jobs.
  • Track the work you complete.
  • Maintain your daily streak.

Complete every requirement on all 10 consecutive days to qualify for the next track.

LEVEL 2🔒 Unlocks after 10 days

Super Warriors

Consistency got you here. Keep growing — the advanced track focuses on:

  • More focused peer accountability.
  • Stronger learning routines.
  • Deeper skill-building.
  • Better-structured progress reviews.

Super Warriors opens once you complete the 10-day track. It's an upcoming track — we'll never pretend a feature exists before it's ready.
