Job search tracker

Stop guessing which job platforms are working.

Record every application with its source — LinkedIn, Naukri, Hirist, a company site — then compare responses and interviews by platform, built from your own data. Put your effort where it's actually paying off.

  • How many jobs did I apply to this week?
  • Which source gives me the most responses?
  • How many applications reached an interview?
  • Which applications need a follow-up today?

This is a manual tracker — you add applications yourself; nothing auto-syncs from LinkedIn, Naukri or other portals. Rates are responses ÷ applications per source from what you record. With no responses yet, you'll see an honest empty state, not an invented percentage.
