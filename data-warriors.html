THE PROBLEM

Your job search is scattered.
So is your prep.

You're already doing the work — applying everywhere and studying hard. But it lives across portals, emails, spreadsheets, and memory. So it's impossible to see what's actually working.

Applications everywhere

You apply through LinkedIn, Naukri, Hirist, company career pages and more — then lose track of:

  • Which companies you applied to, and when.
  • Which portal each opportunity came from.
  • Which applications got a response or an interview.
  • Who needs a follow-up — and which platforms actually work for you.

Preparation you can't measure

You practise SQL, Excel, Python, Power BI, statistics and communication — but it's inconsistent and invisible:

  • You forget what you practised and which topics you covered.
  • You can't see how consistent you've actually been.
  • You don't know where you still need work.
  • There's no single record of your effort over time.
THE SOLUTION

Two trackers. One workspace.

Your job search has a tracker. Your preparation has a tracker. Data Warriors brings both together so you can see your real progress.

A · Job search tracker

Your job search, finally organised

Record every application in one dashboard — company, role, source, job link, dates, status, responses and follow-ups. It answers the questions a scattered search can't:

  • How many jobs did I apply to this week?
  • Which platforms are generating the most responses?
  • How many applications reached an interview?
  • Which applications need a follow-up today?

This is a manual tracker — you add applications yourself. Nothing auto-syncs from LinkedIn, Naukri or other portals, and platform performance is calculated only from what you record.

C · Progress dashboard

A dashboard that shows your progress

Your applications and your preparation, side by side — so you can see momentum and adjust:

  • Applications submitted, responses, and interviews reached.
  • Applications by source and response rate per platform.
  • Practice & learning activity by subject.
  • Daily and weekly consistency, recent activity and upcoming follow-ups.

Every number comes from data you've entered. New here? You'll see a clear empty state with one obvious next step — add your first application or log your first practice session.
