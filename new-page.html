01 / The method

Don't just learn the syntax.
Learn how to think in SQL.

when every step has a purpose.

  2. 02→

    Practise

    Write queries, inspect the output and use a hint when you need one.

  3. 03→

    Combine

    Bring concepts together. Turn a business question into a working query.

    Go deeper with multi-step logic and interview-style SQL challenges.
